Is Zyro really free?
Yeah. Free to use with a limit that refills every 5 hours. No credit card. Pro (₹349/month) unlocks 10× more tokens, mock papers, viva practice, and larger file limits.
Do I need an account?
Nope. Start studying immediately and your chats stay on your device. Sign up free (20 seconds) if you want them synced across phone, laptop, and tablet.
Is this actually built for Indian students?
100%. Built by a 17-year-old B.Tech student in Assam. Every feature is designed around Indian exams — CBSE, ICSE, state boards, JEE, NEET, and university syllabi. Answers follow Indian exam format with marks breakdowns.
What's "exam format"?
Answers structured the way examiners want — a marks estimate, numbered points that hit each marking criterion, and a list of key terms to mention. Not a wall of text.
Can I snap a photo of a question?
Yes. Tap the + button in the input, or use the Snap a question chip. Zyro reads the question and gives you a proper exam-format answer.
What are the study modes?
Chat — normal conversation. Solver — step-by-step solutions with formulas. Socratic — guided learning with questions. Exam prep — marks-ready answers in exam format.
Does Zyro make mistakes?
Yeah, any AI can. Always verify important answers, formulas, and numbers. Zyro is a study tool, not a replacement for your brain.
