Photosynthesis is how green plants make their own food. They use sunlight to turn CO₂ and water into glucose — releasing oxygen as a byproduct.

It happens in the chloroplast, where chlorophyll absorbs blue and red light. Two stages: the light reaction (splits water, makes ATP) and the Calvin cycle (fixes CO₂ into glucose).

Equation: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Where in the cell does photosynthesis happen?
In the chloroplast — thylakoid membranes + stroma.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Zyro free?
Yes. Free tier gives you 25k tokens every 12 hours — enough for a couple of study sets or a full day of chatting. No credit card required to start. Pro (₹349/mo) gets you 150k tokens every 12 hours, web search, and priority generation.
What can I upload?
Any text-based PDF (a textbook chapter, your class notes, a past paper) or a public YouTube lecture with captions. Zyro pulls the content and turns it into notes, flashcards, and a quiz. Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) won't work — see the next question.
Why won't my scanned PDF work?
Scanned PDFs are just photos of pages — there's no text for Zyro to read. You'll get a clear error when this happens. Try a text PDF (downloaded ebooks, class notes typed out) or a YouTube video on the same topic instead.
Do I need an account?
You can browse without one, but to save study sets across devices you'll need to sign up — takes 15 seconds with just an email.
Which exams does it work for?
CBSE, ICSE, state boards, JEE, NEET, CUET, and college syllabi. Zyro adapts the format to your class level and board during onboarding.
Does Zyro make mistakes?
Yeah — like any AI. Always verify important facts, formulas, and numbers against your textbook. Zyro is a study aid, not a replacement for your brain. When it's unsure, it says so.
